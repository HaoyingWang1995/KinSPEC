Suggested rebuttal wording
--------------------------
To further examine whether the protein representation behaves as a target-specific code, we quantified physicochemical feature sharing across all 482 kinase pocket profiles. Across aligned KLIFS positions, different amino-acid identities frequently retained shared PSM physicochemical states. Among non-identical residue pairs, 78.1% shared at least one of the five PSM property-group states, 35.0% shared at least three groups, and 0.0% shared all five groups. Individual physicochemical states were repeatedly reused across kinase targets at the same aligned positions: at the median KLIFS position, 99.6% of valid kinases shared their complete 23-bit PSM state with at least one other kinase. These results show that the MCA input is composed of recurrent, shared physicochemical features rather than mutually exclusive target-specific indicators.
