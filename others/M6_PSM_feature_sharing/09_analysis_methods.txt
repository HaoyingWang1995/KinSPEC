Gap handling
------------
The '-' mapping is 00000000000000000000000. Gaps are excluded from all position-aware pair denominators if either kinase is gapped. They remain 23 zeroes in global 1955-bit Jaccard and Hamming calculations, exactly as in the input representation.

Pairwise metrics
----------------
All C(482,2) = 115,921 unordered kinase pairs were evaluated exactly.
Global active-bit Jaccard = active-bit intersection / active-bit union over 1955 bits.
Global Hamming agreement = equal binary bits / 1955; shared zeroes contribute.
Position PSM Jaccard = active-bit intersection / union for each jointly non-gap position, averaged over valid positions.
AA identity = identical non-gap residues / jointly non-gap positions.
Matching-group quantities compare the five categorical group states at jointly non-gap positions.
Position-level residue comparisons include all unordered kinase pairs jointly non-gap at that position.
The low-identity/high-sharing rank score is (1 - AA pair identity fraction) * mean matching groups among different-AA pairs / 5.

Complete-vector interpretation
------------------------------
Uniqueness of a complete multivariate feature vector does not imply target-identity encoding. The relevant question is whether the constituent aligned physicochemical states are recurrent and shared across kinases.
